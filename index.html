About

Hi, I'm Michael.

I'm a tattoo artist and illustrator based in Tel Aviv, working from a private studio on Shvil HaMeretz. My work is rooted in traditional tattooing — strong lines, solid black, and shading that holds up for decades — with a love for fine, clean detail.

Away from the machine I draw constantly: pencil, ink and digital flash. Every tattoo starts on paper, and every design is made for the person wearing it.
